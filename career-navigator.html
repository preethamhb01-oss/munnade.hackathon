AI Career Navigator

Not everyone knows what they want to become, and that's completely normal.

Sometimes students have interests and skills but don't know how to connect them with a career. The AI Career Navigator helps users explore possible career directions based on their educational background, interests, skills and preferences.

Users can provide basic information about their learning journey and explore suggestions related to different fields. The recommendations can include degree-based careers, government examinations, technical skills, vocational opportunities, creative fields and other career paths. For every suggestion, users can explore the skills they may need, possible learning paths and useful resources.

Our goal is not to decide someone's future for them. It is to help them understand their options and make more informed decisions.
