🛂 Passport

Your Skill Passport

Your abilities are more than just your academic marks.

You may have completed projects, developed technical skills, participated in competitions or learned something independently. The Skill Passport gives users a place to organise and showcase these achievements.

Users can add their skills, projects, certificates and personal accomplishments to build a simple profile of their learning journey. It helps learners recognise their progress and present their abilities in a more organised way. Because every skill you develop is another step towards your future.

Show what you can do, not just what you have studied.
